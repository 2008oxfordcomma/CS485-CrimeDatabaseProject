Data Sources & Methodology

The information displayed on this page is based on the following datasets:

  • Organized Crime Index (OCI): Organized crime criminality, criminal markets, criminal actors, and resilience indicators.
  • Counter Trafficking Data Collaborative (CTDC): Anonymized human trafficking case records used to explore available patterns.

Data availability and coverage may vary between countries, categories, and editions.
